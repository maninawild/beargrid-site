## Selection

`bear-grid-logo.png` is the strongest approved Bear Grid asset and is used
consistently in the sticky header, homepage hero and footer. It has generous
clear space, is rendered from the 270×236 transparent source, and is never
upscaled beyond its intrinsic dimensions.

The current asset set contains a standalone bear mark rather than a separate
image-based wordmark. The company name is therefore paired with the approved
mark in accessible HTML text in the header and footer. Historical third-party
logos remain preserved in the repository and legacy records, but are excluded
from the primary sales experience because the current status and intended
public claim are not confirmed.

The social preview uses `public/og.png`, a dedicated 1200×630 raster asset for
link unfurls. The browser favicon remains `src/app/favicon.ico` at 256×256.
