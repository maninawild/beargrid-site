## Citation readiness standard

A page is citation-ready only when it:

1. Names the entity or concept precisely.
2. Gives a direct definition or answer before elaboration.
3. Separates fact, interpretation, historical claim and recommendation.
4. States method, date, author and material limitations.
5. Uses stable canonical URLs and visible headings matching the subject.
6. Links to primary evidence where available.
7. Can be quoted without losing essential context.
